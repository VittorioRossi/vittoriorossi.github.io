Research

NeurIPS Workshop · 2026
Diffusion Language Models Are Natively Length-Aware
Vittorio Rossi, Giacomo Cirò, Davide Beltrame, Luca Gandolfi, Paul Röttger, Dirk Hovy

Masked diffusion LMs know how long their answer will be before decoding. Reading that from one forward pass and cropping the canvas cuts FLOPs by over 30% on every benchmark, up to 90%, with no training and no significant accuracy loss.

arXiv
MSc thesis · in progress
Do tokenizer preferences transfer from autoregressive to diffusion models?

Diffusion samplers reveal several tokens per step and lose the dependence between them. I test whether that makes them prefer more compressed tokenizers than autoregressive models, across training and sampling budgets.
