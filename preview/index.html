Research

Diffusion Language Models Are Natively Length-Aware
Vittorio Rossi, Giacomo Cirò, Davide Beltrame, Luca Gandolfi, Paul Röttger, Dirk Hovy
NeurIPS 2026 Workshop on Diffusion Language Models · accepted
  • Masked diffusion LLMs encode their output length before decoding. We read it from one forward pass and crop the canvas: zero-shot, no training.
  • >30% fewer FLOPs on every benchmark, up to 90%, with no significant accuracy loss, and gains on 2 of 4 tasks (LLaDA-8B).
